### Aim:
To write the prompts for these following prompt types and evaluate that using any one method:
1. Straightforward Prompts
2. Tabular Format Prompting
3. Preceding Question Prompting and
4. Missing Word Prompting

## Procedure

### 1. Straightforward Prompting

**Description:**  
Straightforward prompting involves giving a direct and simple instruction to an AI model to obtain a specific response.

**Prompt:**

"Explain three applications of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare in simple words."

**Generated Output:**

1. **Disease Diagnosis:** AI helps doctors identify diseases by analysing medical reports and symptoms.
2. **Medical Image Analysis:** AI can assist in examining X-rays, MRI scans, and CT scans to identify possible abnormalities.
3. **Patient Monitoring:** AI systems can monitor patient health information and alert healthcare professionals when attention may be needed.

**Evaluation:**  
The prompt produces a clear, direct, and understandable response without requiring additional instructions.

---

### 2. Tabular Format Prompting

**Description:**  
Tabular prompting instructs the AI model to present information in a structured table with rows and columns.

**Prompt:**

"Create a table showing five applications of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare, their purposes, and one example of each."

**Generated Output:**

| Application | Purpose | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Disease Diagnosis | Helps identify diseases | Analysing symptoms |
| Medical Imaging | Examines medical images | X-ray analysis |
| Patient Monitoring | Tracks patient health | Heart rate monitoring |
| Drug Discovery | Supports medicine research | Identifying drug candidates |
| Robotic Assistance | Assists medical professionals | Robot-assisted surgery |

**Evaluation:**  
The tabular format organises information systematically and makes comparison easier.

---

### 3. Preceding Question Prompting

**Description:**  
Preceding question prompting involves asking a question before giving the main instruction. This provides context and guides the AI towards a more relevant response.

**Prompt:**

"Why is early disease detection important in healthcare? How can Artificial Intelligence help doctors detect diseases at an early stage? Explain its role with suitable examples."

**Generated Output:**

Early disease detection is important because it helps doctors identify health problems before they become more serious. Artificial Intelligence supports early detection by analysing medical images, laboratory reports, and patient information.

For example, AI systems can assist in identifying suspicious patterns in medical scans and help doctors decide whether further examination is required. AI acts as a supporting tool, while medical professionals make the final clinical decisions.

**Evaluation:**  
The preceding question provides context and helps generate a more detailed and relevant explanation.

---

### 4. Missing Word Prompting

**Description:**  
Missing word prompting uses incomplete sentences or blanks that the AI model must complete with appropriate words or information.

**Prompt:**

"Complete the following sentences related to Artificial Intelligence in healthcare:

1. AI stands for __________.
2. AI helps doctors analyse medical __________.
3. AI can assist in examining X-rays and __________ scans.
4. AI-based systems can support early disease __________."

**Generated Output:**

1. Artificial Intelligence
2. Reports
3. MRI
4. Detection

**Evaluation:**  
Missing word prompting is useful for testing basic knowledge, completing statements, and generating short answers.

---

## Overall Evaluation

| Prompting Technique | Output Type | Main Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Straightforward | Direct explanation | Simple and clear responses |
| Tabular Format | Structured table | Easy comparison |
| Preceding Question | Detailed explanation | Provides contextual responses |
| Missing Word | Fill-in-the-blanks | Tests basic knowledge |

## Conclusion

The four prompting techniques were successfully implemented using the scenario of Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare. Each technique generated different types of responses based on the structure and instructions provided in the prompt.

## Result

Thus, different prompting techniques were successfully executed and evaluated using a real-world healthcare scenario.
